Ghana

Ghana is a country located on the west coast of Africa, bordered by Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Togo, and the Gulf of Guinea. Known for its rich culture, friendly people, and political stability, Ghana was the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence in 1957. Its capital city is Accra, and English is the official language. Ghana is famous for its gold, cocoa production, beautiful beaches, historic castles, and diverse traditions that reflect the heritage of many ethnic groups. The country's motto is "Freedom and Justice," and it is recognized as one of Africa's leading democracies.
